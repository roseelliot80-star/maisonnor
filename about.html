About Porchside Birds

Porchside Birds is an independent English-language field guide for people who want to recognize birds from their window, porch, or yard.

How to use the guide

Start with a bird’s shape and one obvious color. Use the filters to narrow the list, then compare the photo with the field marks. Range and appearance can vary with the season, the bird’s age, and its sex.

Sources and corrections

Species profiles point to the Cornell Lab of Ornithology for range maps and more identification examples. The photo credits link to each original photograph. Tell us if you spot a mistake or have trouble using the site.
